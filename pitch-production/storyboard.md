## Act I: The Silent Barrier (0:00 – 0:30)

### Scene 1.1: The Waiting Room (0:00 – 0:12)
- **Visual**: Raking morning light in an outpatient primary clinic in Dhaka. An anxious Deaf patient sits opposite a busy doctor, trying desperately to gesture crushing chest discomfort. The doctor struggles to comprehend.
- **On-Screen Text**: *"59.1% of patients with disabilities in Bangladesh face critical communication barriers at primary health facilities."* (Source: BMJ Health Services Research, 2022).
- **Audio/Narration**: "In healthcare, miscommunication isn't just an inconvenience. It is life-threatening. In Bangladesh, nearly 60 percent of patients with disabilities report that clinicians cannot understand their symptoms."
- **Key Emotion**: Urgent, empathetic, real-world grounding.

### Scene 1.2: The Core Dilemma (0:12 – 0:30)
- **Visual**: Macro montage: Fingertips feeling medical labels in Braille; a phone with inaccessible medical portals; hands signing urgently with no interpreter present.
- **On-Screen Text**: *"When symptoms cannot be communicated, care cannot begin."*
- **Audio/Narration**: "When a stroke, acute myocardial infarction, or severe dengue strikes, seconds matter. But Deaf, blind, and speech-impaired individuals are routinely left isolated in primary clinics."

## Act III: Live Product Demonstration (1:00 – 2:00)

### Scene 3.1: Case 1 — RED Emergency (Sign Language Acute Chest Pain) (1:00 – 1:25)
- **Visual**: Screen recording of Transify Care Wizard.
  - Modality: **Sign Language** chosen.
  - Camera captures gestures: *Chest Pain*, *Difficulty Breathing*.
  - Patient confirms: *"Severe crushing chest pain radiating to left arm for 45 minutes."* Severity slider set to 9. Trajectory: *Worsening*.
  - Patient checks explicit consent.
  - Triage Result instantly flags **URGENCY: RED** (`RULE-EMERG-001`).
  - Emergency banner appears with one-touch direct buttons to **National Emergency 999** and **Health Helpline 16263**.
- **On-Screen Text**: **URGENCY LEVEL: RED** • Acute Coronary Syndrome Flag • Direct 999 Escalation.
- **Audio/Narration**: "Case One: A Deaf patient signs severe chest pain radiating to their left arm. Our deterministic safety engine instantly triggers Urgency RED. No vague chatbot answers. It immediately routes to emergency triage and surfaces Bangladesh National Emergency 999."

### Scene 3.2: Case 2 — YELLOW Urgent (Voice STT Dengue Warning Signs) (1:25 – 1:45)
- **Visual**:
  - Modality: **Voice Hub**.
  - Patient speaks in Bengali/English: *"3 days high fever, severe persistent stomach pain, vomiting."*
  - Structured clarification: Severity 7, Trajectory: *Worsening*.
  - Triage Result: **URGENCY: YELLOW** (`RULE-URGENT-001`).
  - Clinical RAG accordion highlights: *DGHS National Dengue Clinical Management Guidelines (5th Ed)*.
- **Audio/Narration**: "Case Two: A patient with visual impairment describes high fever and severe persistent vomiting. Transify Care identifies dengue warning signs under DGHS National Guidelines, categorizing the case as Urgency YELLOW for prompt same-day physical evaluation."

### Scene 3.3: Case 3 — GREEN Routine (Braille OCR Mild Cough) (1:45 – 2:00)
- **Visual**:
  - Modality: **Braille Document Scanner**.
  - Upload of embossed Braille card. OCR decodes: *"Mild dry cough for 2 days, no fever."*
  - Severity: 2, Trajectory: *Stable*.
  - Triage Result: **URGENCY: GREEN** (`RULE-ROUTINE-001`).
  - Patient taps **"🔊 Listen to Assessment"** (Strict Zero Auto-Audio policy: voice synthesizes only on demand).
- **Audio/Narration**: "Case Three: An embossed Braille note for a mild dry cough is scanned and evaluated as Urgency GREEN. And respecting cognitive load, our strict Zero Auto-Audio architecture speaks only when explicitly requested."
