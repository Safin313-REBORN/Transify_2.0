# Transify Care — 180-Second Competition Narration Script
**Pacing Target**: 130–135 words per minute (Clear, articulate, compassionate, authoritative)  
**Total Word Count**: ~400 words  
**Target Duration**: 180 seconds (03:00)  
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Audio Timing Verification**: Do not rely on word count alone to guarantee the 180-second duration. The speaker must record a dry audio pass with a stopwatch to verify exact phrasing and breath cadence before final video assembly.

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] ACT I: THE SILENT BARRIER

*(0:00)*  
In healthcare, communication is not a luxury. It is a vital sign.  
Yet in Bangladesh, over fifty-nine percent of surveyed individuals with disabilities report dissatisfaction with access to healthcare information and communication (BMC Health Services Research, 2022 — Nawshin Torsha et al.).  

*(0:15)*  
When acute chest pain strikes, or a child suffers dengue complications, seconds decide survival.  
For millions of Deaf, blind, and speech-impaired citizens, navigating primary clinics without accessible communication tools remains a critical barrier to timely care.

---

### [0:30 – 1:00] ACT II: TRANSIFY CARE

*(0:30)*  
This is Transify Care.  
We have extended our multi-modal assistive AI into CareAnywhere: an accessible health intake and safety triage research prototype.  

*(0:45)*  
By combining real-time sign language recognition, optical Braille reading, continuous speech captioning, and natural voice synthesis, Transify Care enables patients to articulate acute symptoms in the modality they know best—whether Bangladesh Sign Language, embossed Braille, or spoken voice.

---

### [1:00 – 1:30] ACT III (PART 1): EMERGENCY & URGENT DEMO

*(1:00)*  
Watch this live.  
A Deaf patient signs severe chest pain radiating down their arm.  
Transify Care parses the sensor landmarks, confirms symptom details, and executes our deterministic safety engine.  

*(1:15)*  
It immediately flags Urgency RED—potential acute coronary syndrome.  
There is no ambiguous chatbot hallucination: it instantly displays emergency contact actions with direct one-touch calls to Bangladesh National Emergency 999 and the 16263 health helpline.

---

### [1:30 – 2:00] ACT III (PART 2): FEVER & BRAILLE DEMO

*(1:30)*  
Next, a febrile patient speaks through our Voice Hub.  
Symptoms of high fever and persistent vomiting match DGHS national guideline warning signs, categorizing the case as Urgency YELLOW for prompt clinical examination.  

*(1:45)*  
Finally, an embossed Braille card describing a mild dry cough is decoded and assigned Urgency GREEN.  
And respecting patient comfort, our strict Zero Auto-Audio policy speaks only when the user explicitly taps "Listen."

---

### [2:00 – 2:30] ACT IV: CLINICAL SAFETY & EVIDENCE LOOKUP

*(2:00)*  
Safety in health AI is non-negotiable.  
Transify Care never offers autonomous medical diagnoses.  
Instead, our prototype keyword-based clinical evidence lookup retrieves protocol passages directly from World Health Organization and DGHS guidelines.  

*(2:15)*  
In our clinician-review workflow prototype, healthcare staff can review incoming queues.  
If a reviewer modifies an urgency level, our system mandates a documented clinical justification—recording a tamper-evident audit trail of human oversight.

---

### [2:30 – 3:00] ACT V: IMPACT & THE HORIZON

*(2:30)*  
In rural community clinics across Bangladesh, internet connectivity is fragile.  
Transify Care includes an offline draft capture prototype—saving unassessed drafts locally and synchronizing once an authenticated connection is restored.  

*(2:45)*  
More than one billion people worldwide live with disabilities.  
Transify Care turns communication from a barrier into a bridge.  
Because healthcare begins when every single patient can finally be understood.
