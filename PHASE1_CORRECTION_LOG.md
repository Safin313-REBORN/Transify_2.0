# Transify — Phase 1 Review & Correction Log

## 1. Document Purpose and Scope

This log records every empirical, architectural, dataset, licensing, and performance metric correction made during the Phase 1 review pass. It serves as a transparent audit trail ensuring research integrity, scientific reproducibility, and strict alignment with real-world accessibility engineering constraints.

| **CORR-01** | **SLR Priority** | Recommended ASL as primary MVP language due to English output specification; relegated BdSL to Phase 3. | **Prioritize Bangladesh Sign Language (BdSL)** for the first MVP. Hierarchical subsets: WLASL100 (2,038 clips), WLASL300 (5,420 clips), WLASL1000 (14,196 clips). | Li et al., WACV 2020 (arXiv:1910.11006); official WLASL GitHub repository documentation. | Eliminates discrepancy between master metadata instances and physically downloadable/filtered video samples. | Ongoing bit-rot of third-party video links on web-scraped datasets. |

| **CORR-06** | **Braille Standard Specification** | Cited general ICEB standards and older 2013 rules. | Explicitly adopted **The Rules of Unified English Braille, Third Edition (2024)** published by the International Council on English Braille (ICEB) as the authoritative decoding specification. | ICEB Official Publication, Third Edition (2024), [iceb.org](https://iceb.org). | The 2024 3rd Edition supersedes the 2013 2nd Edition, introducing revised rules for punctuation, quotation marks, and shortforms. | Ensuring open-source parsing tables (e.g. Liblouis) align with the latest 2024 ICEB rule updates. |
| **CORR-07** | **Braille Architecture Evaluation** | Solely proposed single-stage YOLOv8 direct cell classification without comparative trade-off analysis. | Systematically compared **Approach A (YOLO-based direct 64-class cell classification)** vs. **Approach B (Two-Stage: Cell Localization followed by Cell-Pattern / 6-Dot Classification)**. | **CORR-08** | **Angelina & DSBI Licensing** | Described datasets as open-source without auditing explicit license files. | Clarified that **neither the Angelina Braille Dataset repository nor the DSBI repository includes an official OSI/standard open-source license file**. Neither permits unverified commercialization. | Official GitHub repositories (`yeluo1994/DSBI`, `IlyaOvodov/AngelinaDataset`); OpenDataLab metadata. | Legal compliance. Absence of an explicit license must never be presumed as unrestricted public domain. | Direct formal written permission from authors is required for any commercial deployment. |
| **CORR-09** | **Performance Claims & Metrics Classification** | Reported numbers like "96% Macro F1", "85% Top-1", "1.2 ms latency", "46 FPS", and "98% Braille precision" as established facts. | **Removed all unverified performance claims.** Explicitly classified all metrics into: (1) Measured Experimental Results (None yet); (2) Published External Benchmark Results (attributed to literature); (3) Proposed Research Targets; (4) Unverified Engineering Estimates. Emphasized that **no model has been trained yet by Transify**. | Directive Section 4; experimental validation standards. | Prevent misleading scientific claims before empirical training and held-out validation. | Actual model accuracy will only be established after Phase 2 training on held-out test splits. |
| **CORR-10** | **Continuous Sign Translation Claims** | Implied continuous sign-to-sentence translation could be solved by simple debounce filtering. | Formally separated the 5 tasks: (1) Static alphabet; (2) Dynamic fingerspelling; (3) Isolated word recognition; (4) Continuous SLR (CSLR); (5) Sign-to-English translation (SLT). **Refused to promise continuous sentence translation for MVP**, defining it as a Phase 4 research goal. | Camgoz et al., CVPR 2018 (PHOENIX-Weather 2014T); Koller et al. (TPAMI 2019). | Continuous translation requires aligned parallel video-text corpora and heavy spatial-temporal transformers not supported by MVP scope. | Availability of parallel continuous BdSL sentence video corpora with aligned English translations. |
