1. Assistive Purpose & Nature of Service

Transify provides real-time artificial intelligence tools for Bangladesh Sign Language (BdSL), American Sign Language (ASL), Optical Braille (UEB Grade 1), Speech-to-Text captioning, and Text-to-Speech synthesis. The service is provided as an assistive aid to facilitate interpersonal human communication.

2. Medical & Emergency Disclaimer
