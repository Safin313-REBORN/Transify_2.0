Cookie & Tracking Transparency

Cookie Policy & Tracking Disclosure

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Version: 2.0.0

Transify believes in radical transparency and user agency. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and local storage items are used when you interact with our platform, how they function, and how you maintain total control over your digital footprint.

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies Only

Transify uses only strictly necessary cookies required to maintain authenticated sessions, secure forms against CSRF attacks, and persist your accessibility preferences. We do not use marketing, advertising, cross-site profiling, or data-broker tracking cookies.

2. Complete Cookie & Storage Inventory

Cookie / Key Type Duration Purpose
transify_session Essential (HttpOnly, Secure) Session or 30 Days Cryptographic token authenticating your user session. Never exposed to client-side scripts.
transify_csrf Essential (SameSite=Lax) Session Defense against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) for form submissions.
transify_cookie_consent Preference 1 Year Remembers your consent preference (accepted/declined non-essential) so you are not re-prompted.
transify_theme LocalStorage Preference Persistent Remembers your high-contrast or dark mode visual accessibility selection.

3. Audit of Third-Party SDKs & Zero-Tracking Guarantee

We actively audit and minimize external scripts:

  • Zero Ad Trackers: No Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads, or commercial tracking tags.
  • Privacy-First AI & Vision: Google MediaPipe Holistic tracking runs locally on client hardware via WebAssembly/WebGL, without sending continuous camera telemetry to third parties.
  • Internal Operational Telemetry: Client-side JavaScript errors are logged via our own local API (/api/telemetry/error) for technical debugging without user profiling.

4. Managing and Clearing Cookies

You can manage, restrict, or clear cookies directly through your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Please note that disabling transify_session will prevent you from logging into your account or saving phrases to your personal communication hub.
