Privacy-by-Design

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Version: 2.0.0

At Transify, your privacy and autonomy are fundamental to our mission. This Privacy Policy details our strict adherence to privacy-by-design principles, local ephemeral processing, and international data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA).

1. Ephemeral Biometric & Video Processing

Transify’s core recognition engines (MediaPipe Holistic dual-hand tracking, BdSL sequence analysis, and Optical Braille OBR) process video frames and uploaded camera images strictly in volatile random-access memory (RAM):

  • Webcam video feeds are processed locally on your client machine or transmitted ephemerally to our inference gateway.
  • Zero Video Retention: Video streams and raw camera frames are NEVER recorded, stored, saved to server disks, or used to build biometric surveillance models.
  • Once keypoint coordinates are extracted, raw frame buffers are immediately discarded from memory.

2. Data We Collect and Why

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide authenticated assistive features:

  • Account Credentials: If you register, we store your email, display name, and an Argon2id cryptographic hash of your password. We never have access to plain-text passwords.
  • Accessibility Preferences: Theme settings, font scaling, audio speed, and high-contrast preferences are saved to provide continuity across devices.
  • Communication Hub & Phraseboard: Custom saved phrases and conversation history are stored securely in isolated database partitions associated with your account ID.
  • Session Security: Cryptographically generated session tokens are delivered exclusively through secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookies.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry

Transify does not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information or communication records with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing platforms. There are zero tracking pixels or third-party behavioral fingerprinting scripts on this platform.

4. Your Rights: Access, Export, and Permanent Deletion

In accordance with GDPR and global privacy mandates, you possess full ownership of your data:

  • Right to Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten): You may permanently delete your account and all associated communication records at any time using the "Delete Account" button in your account settings or via our authenticated API.
  • Immediate Purge: When an account is deleted, your user profile, active sessions, preferences, custom phrases, and translation history records are immediately and permanently removed from our production database.

For questions or formal data privacy inquiries, contact our engineering team at privacy@transify.app or general support at support@transify.app, or call our helpline at +1 (800) 555-0199.
