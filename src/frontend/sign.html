ASL & HaGRID Hand Gesture Execution Guide

CALL (HaGRID): Thumb and pinky extended to ear and mouth mimicking a telephone.
LIKE (HaGRID): Thumbs up gesture indicating approval or positive confirmation.
DISLIKE (HaGRID): Thumbs down gesture indicating disapproval or negation.
OK (HaGRID): Thumb and index tip contact forming loop with other three fingers extended.
STOP (HaGRID): Open palm held vertically facing camera with fingers pointing upward.
PEACE (HaGRID): Index and middle fingers extended upward in a 'V' shape.
FIST (HaGRID): Closed clenched fist signifying power or holding.
PALM (HaGRID): Open flat palm facing the camera.
HELLO (ASL): Flat hand moving gently outward from temple in a salute motion.
I_NEED_HELP (ASL): Closed fist with thumb up resting on flat palm lifting upward.
WATER (ASL): Three fingers ('W' shape) tapping chin twice.
FOOD (ASL): Flattened fingertips brought to mouth twice.
