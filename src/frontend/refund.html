Refund & Billing Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Version: 2.0.0

Core Commitment: 100% Free Open-Source Core & Zero Hidden Fees

Transify was founded on the universal right to accessible communication. The core Transify web application, live sign language recognition, optical Braille OCR, speech-to-text, and phraseboard are 100% free and open-source under the MIT License with zero subscription requirements, zero hidden fees, and zero credit card requirements.

1. Free Core Services & Consumer Clarity

Individual users, students, educators, and community members have free and unrestricted access to all core assistive features. There are no recurring fees, trial expiration traps, or surprise paywalls on consumer accounts.

2. Enterprise Cloud, Dedicated Instances & Physical Accessories

For organizations, universities, and enterprise healthcare providers utilizing managed dedicated cloud hosting, high-concurrency dedicated GPU clusters, or specialized tactile Braille accessories:

  • 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: Any enterprise hosting tier or physical assistive accessory may be refunded within thirty (30) calendar days of purchase if it does not satisfy your operational or accessibility needs.
  • Hardware Returns: Physical accessories must be returned in their original packaging and undamaged condition. Transify provides prepaid return shipping labels for defective or non-compliant units.
  • No Hidden Cancellation Penalties: You may cancel cloud subscriptions at any time without early termination penalties or hidden exit charges.

3. Refund Request Procedure

To request a refund or dispute a billing entry:

  1. Send an email to billing@transify.app or support@transify.app with your account email and invoice or order ID.
  2. Our billing team will review and process your request within 2 to 3 business days.
  3. Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your financial institution.
